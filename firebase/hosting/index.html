Try PaiSeN for 14 days

PaiSeN reads the payment slips in your photos and keeps a record of what you spent. Slips are read on your phone, and nothing is uploaded. Before it goes on Google Play, I need 20 friends to test it.

Join on Android Install on iPhone

Free. Your own spending needs no account. For now, slip reading works with K PLUS set to English.

How to join

  1. Join the Google Group

    Use the Google account that's signed in to Play Store on your phone.

    Join paisen-testers

  2. Become a tester

    Tap Become a tester on the page that opens. If it says the app isn't available, wait a few minutes after joining the group and open it again.

    Open the tester page

  3. Install from Google Play

    Then keep PaiSeN installed for 14 days and open it now and then.

    Open in Google Play

Use the same Google account for all three steps.

There's no App Store test yet, so on iPhone you install PaiSeN with SideStore and your own Apple ID.

  1. Set up SideStore

    Follow SideStore's own guide. You need a computer once.

    Open SideStore's guide

  2. Download PaiSeN

    On the iPhone, open the newest release and download PaiSeN-<version>.ipa.

    Open releases on GitHub

  3. Add it in SideStore

    Open My Apps, tap + and pick the file.

  4. Refresh once a week

    Open SideStore and tap Refresh All. A free Apple ID signs an app for 7 days, and PaiSeN won't open after that until you refresh.

On iPhone, Sign in with Apple and notifications don't work yet. For shared groups, sign in with Google.

What you'll test

  • Today

    What you spent this month and your latest purchases.

  • Reports

    Spending by week, month, 3 months, 6 months or a year.

  • Quick add

    Type or say “coffee 60” in English, Thai or Burmese.

  • Shared

    What you're owed and what you owe, across groups.

  • A shared group

    Split a trip or a bill, and settle up with a PromptPay QR.

  • Settings

    Photo folders, background import and your categories.

What stays on your phone

  • Slips are read on the phone

    ML Kit on Android and Apple Vision on iPhone read them. Nothing is uploaded.

  • No account, no ads, no analytics

    Your own spending needs no sign-in.

  • Shared groups only when you share

    Only a group you share is sent, and only after you sign in.

What testing asks of you

  • PaiSeN reads K PLUS slips for now, and K PLUS has to be set to English. With another bank, add expenses by hand or with Quick add, and tell me which bank you use.
  • Keep PaiSeN installed for 14 days.
  • Add a few expenses, or let it read your slips.
  • When something breaks or confuses you, take a screenshot and send it to the friend who shared this page.
  • Use GitHub? You can also open an issue.
